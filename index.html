Masks, by material.

Cryptomatte masks are now part of Lumina.

Click a material, an object or the sky in the viewport and Lumina builds the mask for you. Grade the glazing without touching the facade, warm up one room, or pull a client's product forward. Every mask stays a layer you can adjust later.
